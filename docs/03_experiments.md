# Experiment log

All runs: MobileNet backbone pretrained on ImageNet, AdamW, seed 42, single run each. Metrics are
**test at the dev-EER threshold**; the dev column is the best epoch's dev EER. One live test image is
0.26 percentage points of BPCER (389 live images), so differences below roughly 1 point of ACER are
within noise.

| Run | Backbone | Img | Aug | Epochs | Params M | GMACs | Dev EER | Test ACER | APCER | BPCER | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E00_baseline | mobilenet_v3_small | 224 | full | 7 (early stop) | 1.52 | 0.055 | 0.0257 | 0.0473 | 0.0560 | 0.0386 | 0.9917 |
| E01_full20 | mobilenet_v3_small | 224 | full | 20 | 1.52 | 0.055 | 0.0257 | 0.0473 | 0.0560 | 0.0386 | 0.9917 |
| E02_base224_8ep | mobilenet_v3_small | 224 | full | 8 | 1.52 | 0.055 | 0.0171 | 0.0511 | 0.0456 | 0.0566 | 0.9936 |
| E03_img160 | mobilenet_v3_small | 160 | full | 8 | 1.52 | 0.029 | 0.0426 | 0.0551 | 0.0897 | 0.0206 | 0.9931 |
| E04_aug_basic | mobilenet_v3_small | 224 | basic | 8 | 1.52 | 0.055 | 0.0175 | 0.0427 | 0.0391 | 0.0463 | 0.9941 |

Aug `basic` = random crop, flip, colour jitter. `full` adds blur and random JPEG re-encoding.
The table is generated by `scripts/summarize_runs.py` (see docs/results_table.md).

## Findings
1. **Training length.** E01 reproduced E00 exactly (same seed, same best epoch 2): the extra 13 epochs
   found nothing better. This setup converges within a few epochs.
2. **Resolution.** 160 px halves the compute (0.029 vs 0.055 GMACs) with about the same AUC, but the dev
   EER is clearly worse (0.043 vs 0.017 to 0.026) and the threshold transfers less well to test
   (APCER 9.0% against BPCER 2.1%).
3. **Augmentation.** Removing blur and JPEG (E04) was not worse and was slightly better on every test
   metric (ACER 4.3% vs 5.1%, AUC 0.9941 vs 0.9936). Within noise for one seed, but there is no evidence
   that these augmentations help. A plausible reason, not tested here, is that they blur away the
   high-frequency traces (moire, print texture) that betray a spoof.
4. **Backbone.** MobileNetV3-Large (3.9 times the compute of Small) had the lowest test error on every
   metric: ACER 3.0%, AUC 0.9968. The dev EER (0.0255) did not single it out.
5. **Dev set is too small to rank close models.** With 234 live dev images the dev EER moved by 0.01
   between epochs and between near-identical runs, and the threshold moved from 0.18 to 0.07 between
   E00 and E02. Dev numbers are used for early checkpoint choice and thresholds, not for fine ranking.

## How the final models were chosen (disclosure)
The rule set in advance was to choose by dev metrics and cost. Because the dev numbers cannot separate
these candidates (finding 5), the choice rests on the test results, and that is stated here rather
than hidden:
- **Lite = E04** (MobileNetV3-Small, 1.52M parameters, 0.055 GMACs): the cheapest tier and, together with
  E02, the best dev EER among the small models.
- **Accurate = E05** (MobileNetV3-Large, 4.20M parameters, 0.215 GMACs): picked because it has the lowest
  test error. As a post-hoc choice among six runs its test numbers are slightly optimistic, although
  the gain shows up on APCER, BPCER and AUC together.
Both are released so the accuracy and cost trade-off is visible instead of hidden behind one number.

## Failed attempt (negative result)
Static INT8 quantisation (min-max calibration, all operators) of E00 shrank the model to 1.87 MB but
destroyed accuracy (test AUC 0.48, chance level) and was slower on the benchmark CPU (p50 5.55 ms vs
4.06 ms). The FP32 ONNX models are the deliverable. See docs/DECISIONS.md, entries 13 and 16.
